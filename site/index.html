FAQ

Good questions.

Where is the game hosted?

We host it for you on a fast static web host and send you the link. It stays online for the whole holiday season. On Standard you also get embed code to place it on your own site.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. Every game is made for phone screens first and also works on tablets and computers, with touch, mouse or keyboard.

Do players need to download an app?

No. The game opens in the web browser from a link or QR code. Nothing to install, no sign-up.

Are there ads in the game?

No. The only brand in your game is yours.

Do you collect players' data?

No. The games do not use cookies, trackers or analytics, and they do not ask for names or emails. High scores are kept on the player's own device.

Can we put it in our email or newsletter?

Yes, as a link or a QR code. Email apps cannot run games inside the email itself, so you add a button or image that opens the game. We can send you a ready-made image for it.

What is the deadline for Christmas delivery?

Order by December 15 and you will have your link before Christmas. Basic and Standard orders are delivered within 48 hours; Custom games take 1 to 2 weeks, so start those earlier.

How are the games made?

Games are built by a small studio using AI-assisted tools and hand-tested.
